# Oritic panel: kaise use karein

Ye wahi guide hai jo panel ke andar **Menu → Guide** me hai.

## 1. Ek din ka poora kaam (sabse pehle ye padho)

1. Shaam ko: customers ke call / WhatsApp se jo badlav aaye, wo Delivery sheet me likho. Phir "Sab confirm karo" dabao.
2. Demand page se kal ka order supplier ko WhatsApp karo. Delivery sheet ke "Loading sheet" se dekho kis boy ko kitni bottle deni hai.
3. Subah: boys apne phone me "Aaj list" se har ghar pe ✓ lagate hain, khali bottle wapas likhte hain, payment lete hain.
4. Din me office: Payments me aaye paise likho, boy ne cash diya to Team & cash me "Mila ✓" karo, kharcha / maal Kharcha page me likho.
5. Raat ko: Dashboard ka "Dhyan dein" box dekho. Jo laal hai use theek karo. Settings me "Pura backup" le lo.

> Dhyan: Delivery sheet subah 9 baje ke baad apne aap agle din pe khulti hai. Ye time Settings → Office tools me badal sakte ho.
>
> Dhyan: Kahin se bhi Ctrl + K dabao aur customer ka naam likho. Sabse tez tareeka.
>
> Dhyan: Bayen taraf ka menu sirf icons me simta rehta hai, mouse le jao to khulta hai. Hamesha khula chahiye to menu me upar 📌 dabao (har computer apni pasand yaad rakhta hai).

## 2. Dashboard

**Kahan:** Menu → Dashboard

1. "Dhyan dein" box: jo kaam ruka hai wo yahan dikhta hai (order confirm baaki, delivery pe ✓ nahi, cash handover baaki, limit se zyada baki, bottle nahi lauti). Kisi line pe click karo to wahi page khulta hai.
2. Uske neeche is mahine ka hisab: Sale, Paisa aaya, Maal + kharcha, Bachat (andaza), Customers se lena.
3. "Cash kiske paas" me har insaan ke haath ka cash, aur "Bottles" me doodh ke type se kitni bottle bahar hain.
4. Neeche aaj ka load, boy-wise table, kal ka anumaan aur 7 din ka graph pehle jaisa hai.

> Dhyan: Bachat andaza hai: sale me udhaar wala maal bhi gina hai, aur bacha stock nahi gina.

## 3. Delivery sheet (order lena, confirm karna, delivery mark karna)

**Kahan:** Menu → Delivery

1. Upar date dekho. "Aaj ki date" safed, pichli date peeli, agli date hari dikhti hai. ‹ › ya [ ] se din badlo, "Pichli / Aaj ki / Agli" se seedha jao.
2. Har customer ki line me roz ka order pehle se bhara hai. Customer ne badlav bola to us dabbe me number likho. Enter = neeche wala customer, Tab = agla product.
3. Line ke shuru me ✓ dabao = order confirm. Quantity 0 karke ✓ = us din doodh nahi jayega. Sab theek ho to upar "Sab confirm karo".
4. Delivery ho gayi to Status column me "✓ Diya", nahi gaya to "Skip". Galti ho to ↺ se wapas pending.
5. "Wapas" column me likho kitni ½L aur 1L khali bottle wapas aayi.
6. Shahad, dahi, gur jaise product: "+ Product jodo" se. Jo product roz jaate hain unka apna column bana lo (⋯ Aur → ⚙ Columns → Product ka apna column), phir bas quantity likhni hai.
7. Kai customers ek saath: line ke aage ka checkbox tick karo (Shift + click = poori range). Neeche kaali patti aayegi: Confirm, ✓ Diya, Skip, quantity, product, comment sab ek saath.
8. ⋯ Aur → ⚙ Columns: kaunsa column pehle, kaunsa baad me, kaunsa chhupana hai. ↑ ↓ se aage-peeche karo. "Customer ka baki" column bhi yahin se on hota hai.
9. "Loading sheet": har boy ko Cow / Buffalo / Gir ki kitni ½L aur 1L bottle aur kaunsa saamaan load karna hai. Copy karke WhatsApp ya Print.
10. ⋯ me: route ka order badlo, Auto-fill dobara, chhote rows (compact).

> Dhyan: Pichli date pe kuch bhi badloge to popup poochhega aur badlav ka record ban jayega (Settings → Pichli date ke badlav).
>
> Dhyan: Laal box "17 order abhi confirm nahi" pe click karo to sirf wahi customers dikhte hain.
>
> Dhyan: Tiles me har doodh ke neeche bottle ka hisab likha hai, jaise "18×1L + 7×½L".

## 4. Aaj list (delivery boy wali screen)

**Kahan:** Menu → Aaj list

1. Boy apne naam aur PIN se login karta hai, use sirf apne customers dikhte hain.
2. Ghar pe doodh diya to ✓, nahi diya to Skip. Naam pe tap karo to address, map, call, instructions aur "kitni bottle wapas li" dikhta hai.
3. Customer ne paisa diya to usi screen se "Receive payment".
4. Customer ne kuch kaha (kal 2L chahiye) to "Comment bhejo". Office ko Delivery sheet me dikh jayega.

> Dhyan: Office bhi yahi list dekh sakta hai, par office ke liye Delivery sheet zyada tez hai.

## 5. Customers (list, grid, Excel import)

**Kahan:** Menu → Customers

1. Grid view Excel jaisa hai: kisi bhi dabbe me seedha likho, Ctrl + S se save. Badle hue dabbe peele, galat wale laal dikhte hain.
2. Naya customer: "+ 1 khali row" ya "+ 10 rows", naam / boy / schedule bharo, Ctrl + S. Mobile zaroori nahi.
3. Excel se copy karke grid me paste kar sakte ho (kai dabbe ek saath).
4. Bahut saare naye customer: "Excel import" → Template download → bhar ke upload → preview me galti dekho → Import.
5. Kai customers ka boy / rate / quantity ek saath badalna: checkbox tick karo, neeche ki patti se lagao, phir Save.

> Dhyan: Line ke number (↗) pe click karo to us customer ka poora account khulta hai.

## 6. Customer ka account (khata, rate, discount, chhutti)

**Kahan:** Customers me number ↗ pe click · ya Ctrl + K → naam → Enter

1. Upar: kul baki, is mahine ka bill, pichla payment, bottles, abhi ke rate. ← → se agla / pichla customer, Esc se band.
2. "Order lo": seedha Delivery sheet me is customer ki line khul jaati hai. Phone pe order lete waqt kaam aata hai.
3. Khata tab: bank passbook jaisa. Har din ka doodh × rate, payment, discount, aur chalta hua baki. Mahina ya tareekh chuno. "Statement Excel" / "Print".
4. Rate kam karna (customer chhod raha ho): "Rate badlo / Discount" → naya rate likho → "Kab se lagu" chuno → Kyun → Save. Purane din purane rate pe rehte hain.
5. Ek baar ka discount / extra charge / baki band karna: "Adjustment".
6. Chhutti: Delivery calendar tab → "Chhutti lagao" → kab se, kab tak. Un dino customer sheet me nahi aayega, baad me apne aap shuru.
7. Payments tab: har payment, kis tarah (Cash / UPI) aur kisne liya. Bottles tab: kab di, kab wapas aayi.

> Dhyan: Pichli tareekh se rate badloge to panel poochhega ki purane delivered din ka bill bhi badalna hai ya nahi.

## 7. Vasooli (kis se kitna lena hai)

**Kahan:** Menu → Vasooli

1. Upar ke dabbe: kul lena hai, 0–15 din, 16–30 din, 30+ din (pichle payment ko kitne din hue).
2. Filter: 15+ din, 30+ din, ₹1,000+, Limit paar, Paused / chhod gaye, Advance.
3. "Yaad dilao": WhatsApp khulta hai, message pehle se likha hota hai (baki, pichla payment, UPI). Aap bas Send dabao.
4. "Agla yaad dilao": ek ke baad ek sabko. Jisko aaj bhej diya uske aage "aaj" likha aata hai.
5. Paisa aa gaya to usi line me "Payment lo".

> Dhyan: Udhaar ki limit Settings → Office tools me daalo. Limit paar wale customer Delivery sheet me bhi laal nishaan ke saath dikhenge.

## 8. Bills

**Kahan:** Menu → Bills

1. Mahina chuno, phir Pura mahina / 1–15 / 16–end.
2. Customer pe click = bill ka message. "WhatsApp pe bhejo" ya "Bill copy karo".
3. "WhatsApp queue": sabke bill ek ke baad ek bhejne ke liye.
4. "Excel (.xlsx)": poori list download.

## 9. Payments (paisa lena)

**Kahan:** Menu → Payments · ya kahin se bhi P dabao

1. Ek payment: "+ Payment lo" → customer → amount → Cash / UPI → "Kisne liya" → Save (do baar dabana hota hai, galti se bachne ke liye).
2. Kai payments ek saath: "Bulk payment entry" me naam likho → Enter → amount → Enter → agli line. Aakhir me Ctrl + S.
3. "Kisne liya" zaroori hai: cash jisne liya uske haath me gina jata hai (Team & cash).

> Dhyan: Amount khaali aata hai. Pura baki lena ho to "Pura baki bharo" dabao.

## 10. Team & cash (boys aur office ka hisab)

**Kahan:** Menu → Team & cash

1. Table me har insaan: haath me kitna cash, aaj kitna liya, aaj kitni delivery, is mahine ka KM.
2. Boy ne office ko cash diya: "+ Cash handover" → Kisne diya, Kisko diya, Amount → Save.
3. Boy apne phone se bhi likh sakta hai (Payments → "Office ko cash diya"). Tab yahan "Ye cash office ko mila ya nahi?" me aayega: ginke "✓ Mila" dabao. Kam mila to utna hi likho.
4. Cash bank me jama kiya: "+ Bank me jama".
5. Naya boy ya office wala: "+ Naya member" → naam, kaam, phone, joining date, PIN, gaadi (apni / company ki), fuel (petrol / EV), aur salary / incentive ke niyam.
6. Kisi ke naam pe click = uska account: Profile, Cash khata (har rupaya kahan se aaya, kahan gaya), Collection (aur kisko paisa wapas diya), Delivery (din-wise ya customer-wise), Bottles (di, wapas, 7+ din purani), KM & petrol, Hisab / incentive, Salary / advance.
7. Hisab / incentive: salary + bhatta + har litre + har ghar + KM + trip, minus jo salary / advance diya = dena baaki. Bonus ya chhutti ke liye "Sudhaar".
8. Kisi ne kaam chhod diya: uske Profile me "Kaam chhod diya". Login band, purana hisab "Purane member" me rehta hai.
9. Salary ya advance dena: account me "Salary / advance do". Ye Kharcha me bhi jud jata hai.

> Dhyan: UPI seedha company ke account me jata hai, isliye kisi ke haath me nahi gina jata.
>
> Dhyan: Customer ko paisa wapas dena (advance lautana): customer ka account → Adjustment → "Paisa wapas diya", aur kisne diya chuno. Cash me diya to uske haath ka cash ghatega.
>
> Dhyan: Naya mahina fresh shuru karna ho to neeche "Hisab kis date se gino" me tareekh daalo.
>
> Dhyan: Office ke logon ke naam Settings → Office tools → Office staff me daalo.

## 11. Kharcha (maal khareedna, kharcha, supplier)

**Kahan:** Menu → Kharcha

1. "Maal khareeda": jo customers ko dene ke liye khareeda (doodh, ghee, shahad, dahi, gur). Cheez, quantity, rate likho, amount apne aap. Supplier aur paisa kaise diya chuno.
2. Udhaar pe liya to "Udhaar (baad me denge)" chuno. Wo supplier ke khate me jud jayega.
3. "Kharcha": petrol, salary, packing, rent. Category chuno, amount likho. Salary / advance ho to "Kisko diya" me naam chuno.
4. "Supplier ko payment": purana udhaar chukaya.
5. Upar "… ka hisab": mahine ki sale, maal, kharcha, bachat (andaza), category-wise kharcha, Excel.
6. "Suppliers": har supplier ko kitna dena baki hai. "Khata" se poori list, "Payment" se bhugtan.

> Dhyan: Cash se diya to jisne diya uske haath ka cash apne aap ghat jata hai.
>
> Dhyan: Maal me "Cow milk / Buffalo milk / Gir cow milk / Chaach" litre me likhoge to neeche "Doodh ka hisab" dikhega: kitna khareeda, kitna order tha, kitna bacha.

## 12. Demand (kal kya chahiye, supplier ko bhejna)

**Kahan:** Menu → Demand

1. Upar "Supplier / godown ko order bhejo": tareekh chuno, list apne aap banti hai (doodh litre + bottle, aur saath ka saamaan).
2. List me kuch badalna ho (thoda extra doodh) to wahin likh do.
3. "WhatsApp pe bhejo" ya "Copy karo". Supplier ka number Kharcha → Suppliers me daala ho to uska naam chun sakte ho.
4. Neeche aane wale 7 / 15 / 30 din ke other products ki zaroorat dikhti hai.

> Dhyan: Panel khud kisi ko message nahi bhejta. WhatsApp khulta hai, Send aap dabate ho.

## 13. Products (shahad, dahi, gur, ghee ki list)

**Kahan:** Menu → Products

1. Naya product: naam, unit (pc / kg / pack), rate → "+ Product jodo". Alag variety alag naam se (Shahad 250g, Shahad 500g).
2. "Sheet column" tick karo to Delivery sheet me us product ka apna column ban jata hai.
3. Rate badla to line me likh ke Save. Customer ko pichli baar jis rate pe diya tha wahi rate uske liye apne aap aata hai.

## 14. Bottles

**Kahan:** Menu → Bottles

1. Upar: customers ke paas kul kitni bottle, Cow / Buffalo / Gir / Chaach aur ½L / 1L ke hisaab se.
2. "7+ din se nahi lautayi": jin se bottle maangni hai.
3. Bottle dena apne aap judta hai (delivery pe ✓ lagte hi). Wapasi Delivery sheet ke "Wapas" column me ya boy ke phone se likho.
4. Galti theek karni ho to − / + . Customer ke naam pe click = uski bottle ki poori history.

> Dhyan: Doodh ke type ka hisab aise banta hai: jo bottle pehle di thi wahi pehle wapas maani jaati hai.

## 15. Meter (gaadi ka KM)

**Kahan:** Menu → Meter

1. Boy duty shuru aur khatam karte waqt apne phone se meter ki photo leta hai. Time apne aap aa jata hai.
2. Office: photo dekh ke "Start KM" aur "End KM" likho. Total KM apne aap nikalta hai. Save dabao to incentive ban jata hai.
3. Photo na ho to time aur KM haath se bhi likh sakte ho.
4. Do chakkar lagaye (jaise pehle tempo se gaanv se doodh, phir delivery) to "Trip / chakkar" me likho.
5. Petrol bharwaya ya EV charge kiya: "⛽ Petrol / charging" → litre / unit, ₹, kisne paise diye. Ye Kharcha me bhi judta hai.
6. Neeche mahine ka KM, incentive aur petrol, aur petrol ka ₹ per KM. Boy ke naam pe click = din-wise.

> Dhyan: ₹ per km har boy ka apna (Team & cash → naam → Profile). Na ho to Settings wala rate lagta hai.

## 16. Settings aur backup

**Kahan:** Menu → Settings

1. Company ka naam, UPI, bill ke neeche ki line, boys ke naam aur PIN, default rate.
2. Office tools: "Aaj ki date" kab badle, Paneer / Ghee rate, Udhaar limit, Kharcha ki categories, Office staff.
3. Database: "Pura backup" roz le lo. "Backup se wapas laao" se purana data wapas aata hai.
4. Abhi panel "Local mode" me hai: data sirf isi browser me hai. Sab phones pe chalane ke liye Firebase jodna hoga (README dekho).

## 17. Keyboard shortcuts

**Kahan:** Kahin bhi ? dabao

1. Ctrl + K: customer / page / kaam dhoondo. Enter = account, Shift + Enter = bill, Alt + Enter = payment.
2. g phir d = Delivery, g c = Customers, g p = Payments, g b = Bills, g h = Dashboard.
3. Delivery sheet: Enter / ↓ agla customer, Ctrl + Enter confirm, [ ] din badlo.
4. Customers grid aur Bulk payment: Ctrl + S save.
